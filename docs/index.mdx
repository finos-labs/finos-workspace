# FINOS Workspace

FINOS Workspace is an open, self-hosted analytics workspace for financial institutions. You connect your own data backends, arrange their data in dashboards of widgets, and save dashboards as apps that others can open. An AI Copilot reads your dashboards and answers questions about them, and the MCP Companion lets your own AI tools work with them.

New here? Start with the [Quick Start](quick-start.mdx), then connect your AI tool through the [MCP Companion](mcp/index.mdx).
