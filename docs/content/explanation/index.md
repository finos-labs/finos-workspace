# Explanation

Explanation gives the background: how the parts fit together and why they are built the way they are. It discusses alternatives and trade-offs, and compares parts to systems you may already know. It has no steps and no exhaustive lists.

To learn by doing, use the [tutorials](/tutorials). For a task, use the [how-to guides](/how-to). For exact details, see the [reference](/reference).
